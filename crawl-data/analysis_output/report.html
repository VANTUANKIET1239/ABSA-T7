How to interpret these results

  • These are collected review records. The Tiki crawler uses rating quotas, so rating shares should not be interpreted as representative market satisfaction.
  • All rows are retained. Repeated text and duplicate rows are counted in the quality table, not removed.
  • Platform names and brand case/whitespace variants are normalized in the analysis only. Missing brands remain unknown, and related brand names are not inferred or merged.
  • Character length includes whitespace and punctuation. Token length counts whitespace-separated tokens; it is not Vietnamese linguistic word segmentation.
  • Product counts use nonblank product URLs within each group. They do not identify the same product across platforms.
  • Shares use all records; rating percentages use valid ratings within each group. Source missingness is measured before calculated fields are added.
  • Dates are parsed without timezone conversion. 141 missing or unparseable dates are excluded from the monthly chart and listed in unparsed_dates.csv. Relative dates need a crawl date to resolve. Future-date checks use the computer's current date.
  • Prices and discounts are omitted from comparisons because listing size, currency, and discount units have not been validated.
